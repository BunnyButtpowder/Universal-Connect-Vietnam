Word templates for tour registration emails (server-side generation).

Required files in this folder:
- VN_template.docx
- IUC_Invoice VAT_UNIVERSITY_template.docx

Copy from `New Version Templates/` when updating templates.

New template placeholders (handled by documentProcessor.js):
- Registration: {{PARTY_A_*}} (from AUTO-FILLED), {{TOUR_CITIES}}, {{STANDARD_PRICE}},
  {{STANDARD_DEADLINE}}, {{EARLY_DEADLINE}}, [FINAL PRICE], signature fields
- Invoice: [UNIVERSITY NAME], [Head office address], [FINAL PRICE], [NO], [TODAY],
  (CITY NAME) in product line, [FINAL PRICE * 108%]
